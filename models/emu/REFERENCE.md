# Mumbai suburban EMU: what the model has to match

The model follows the Bombardier/Medha non-AC rakes that run most Mumbai
locals today (Western Railway's white-and-purple rakes, Central Railway's
pink ones), from photos of the rakes in service. The numbers come from
Indian Railways' EMU references (IRIMEE "Basics of EMU", Central Railway's
AC EMU book).

## Size

- Coach 20.73 m long, 3.66 m wide, on 1,676 mm broad gauge.
- Floor about 1.15 m above rail; roof top about 4.0 m.
- Two bogies per coach, 2.9 m wheelbase, ~0.95 m wheels.

## Driving cab front

- Nearly flat and upright, with strongly rounded vertical corners and a
  rounded top edge running into the roof.
- Painted yellow all over (Central Railway's newest rakes paint it in the
  line colour instead).
- Two large windscreens, rounded rectangles in black surrounds, split by a
  yellow centre pillar. One wiper each.
- An amber LED destination board above each windscreen.
- A black headlight pod with twin lamps on top of the front, at the roof
  centre, with a horn beside it.
- A full-width black band across the lower front, with a round lamp
  cluster near each corner (red tail lamp and white marker lamp).
- A white square plate with a red X, the tail-end marker, on the lower
  front.
- Below the body: a grey buffer beam with two round side buffers, a
  centre coupler, and a slotted lifeguard close to the rails.
- Vertical grab handles at both front corners.

## Sides

- White body.
- A wide band in the line colour below the windows, running the length of
  the coach, and a thin stripe in the line colour above the windows.
- Three open doorways a side (non-AC rakes have no door leaves), dark
  inside, with a vertical grab pole in the middle and the line colour
  framing the opening.
- Windows in pairs between the doorways, with grey louvred shutters and
  vertical safety bars.
- The driver's cab has a narrow side window and a cab door, and a
  passenger doorway sits right behind the cab.

## Roof and underframe

- Light grey roof curving down to the sides, with a rain gutter along each
  edge and low rectangular ventilator boxes along the middle.
- Dark underframe with equipment boxes between the bogies; black bogies.

## AC rakes

From photos of the ICF-built AC EMU in Mumbai (2017 onwards). The AC
coaches share the non-AC cab shape, bogies, underframe, pantograph and
buffer gear; what differs:

- Unpainted stainless sides. A blue band low down with a thin red line
  under it and a red stripe just above it; a blue stripe along the top of
  the side. Round the cab corner the band and stripe slope down to the
  front.
- Cab front: the yellow panel has a thick red border, inside a stainless
  surround, over a blue band across the bottom of the front. Blue upper
  headlight housing, blue horns at both roof corners, rectangular lamps at
  the ends of the black band with two pairs of red lights between them,
  "ICF" in blue under the right-hand windscreen. No marker plates and no
  train number on the front; stainless bumper.
- Closed stainless sliding doors (no band across them), wide sealed
  windows in black rubber, no grilles or shutters, no line lettering.
- On the cab car, behind the driver's door: a column of louvres and a
  blue panel running down into the band.
- Roof-mounted AC packages with two round condenser fans each: two per
  coach, one on the motor coach beside the pantograph gear.
